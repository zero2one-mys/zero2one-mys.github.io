## QR Code Wi-Fi Share: Easy Connection for Guest Wi-Fi and Cafe Wi-Fi!

"QR Code Wi-Fi Share" is a convenient app that converts Wi-Fi connection information, URLs, and text into QR codes, making sharing and connecting easy. No more manual password entry or complicated settings. You can provide secure and speedy Wi-Fi to friends, family, and guests at cafes and events.

## Key Features: Make Wi-Fi Sharing and QR Code Creation More Convenient

- **Automatic QR code generation from Wi-Fi information**
  Simply enter the SSID and password to instantly create a QR code for Wi-Fi connection. Automatic connection is completed by simply scanning it.
- **Share URLs and text as QR codes**
  Not only Wi-Fi information, but also website URLs and arbitrary text can be converted into QR codes. Information sharing becomes significantly smoother.
- **Save and share created QR codes**
  Generated QR codes can be saved as images and easily shared via SNS or email. Once created, they can be reused anytime.
- **Customize the app with various themes**
  Change the app's appearance by choosing your favorite design from a rich selection of color themes, including default, dark mode, red, and pink.
- **Multi-language support**
  Supports multiple languages, including Japanese, allowing users worldwide to use it comfortably.
- **Ad-free option for even more comfort**
  By using the in-app subscription, you can hide ads and use the app with greater focus.

## Benefits of this app: Zero stress for Wi-Fi connection

- **Fast and easy Wi-Fi sharing**
  Eliminates the hassle of verbally conveying long and complex passwords; simply scan the QR code for instant connection.
- **High security and privacy protection**
  No need to directly teach Wi-Fi passwords, and data is processed within the device, reducing the risk of information leakage.
- **Useful in all scenes**
  Can be used in various situations, such as guest Wi-Fi at home, customer Wi-Fi at cafes and stores, and temporary Wi-Fi provision at event venues.
- **Smooth information sharing**
  Text information such as websites and contact details can also be easily shared via QR codes, which is useful in business settings.

## Recommended for these people:

- Those who want to easily share Wi-Fi when inviting friends home.
- Those who want to simplify guest Wi-Fi provision at cafes and small offices.
- Those who want to provide stress-free internet connection to visitors at event venues.
- Those who want to smartly share URLs and text via QR codes.
- Those who want to comfortably use the app without ads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 📢 Can I automatically get the Wi-Fi name (SSID)?
Yes, you can automatically get the connected Wi-Fi name (SSID). However, due to Android OS security reasons, access permission to detailed location information and ad viewing are required. Please enter the password manually.

### 🎨 Can I change the app's theme?
Yes, you can change to your preferred theme from the theme settings.

### 🌐 Can I hide in-app ads?
Yes, by using the in-app subscription, you can remove in-app ads.

## How to Set Up / How to Use

1. **Create QR code for URL or text**: Launch the app, enter the URL or text in the "Input" tab, and tap the "Create QR Code" button.
2. **Create QR code for Wi-Fi**: In the "Wi-Fi" tab, enter the Wi-Fi name (SSID) and password. The SSID can also be automatically obtained by tapping the "Get Connected Wi-Fi" button.
3. **Share QR code**: The generated QR code can be saved as an image or shared to other apps using the "Share" button.
