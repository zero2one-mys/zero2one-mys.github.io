## Tap Number: Fast Number Tapping! Boost Concentration with Speed Brain Training Game

"Tap Number" is a simple yet addictive brain training speed tapping game. Tap numbers and alphabets in sequence at high speed to push your reflexes and concentration to their limits. With a wide range of difficulties enjoyable for all ages, and a global ranking system to compete with players worldwide, daily brain training becomes even more fun.

## Key Features: Diverse Modes and Challenges to Activate Your Brain

- **Number Mode & Alphabet Mode**: In addition to tapping numbers starting from 1, challenge yourself by tapping alphabets starting from A. Utilize your brain fully with different stimuli.
- **Wide Range of Difficulty Settings**: From 3x3 beginner stages to 11x11 super hard stages, you can challenge yourself according to your level.
- **Global Ranking Function**: Compete with friends and players worldwide for high scores and test your abilities.
- **Perfect for Spare Time**: Play quickly in short bursts, making brain training easy during commutes or short waiting times.
- **High Score Recording and Updating**: The exhilaration of updating your personal best motivates continuous training.
- **User Name Registration/Change Function**: Freely set and change the name displayed in the rankings.
- **Sound ON/OFF Setting**: Easily toggle game sounds when you need to concentrate or play in public places.
- **SNS Share Function**: Share your achieved high scores and game fun with friends to enjoy brain training together!
- **In-app Ad Removal**: Remove ads with a monthly subscription for a more comfortable playing environment.
- **Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Inquiries**: Clearly states app safety and terms of use, with comprehensive user support.

## Benefits of this app: Get Smarter While Playing!

- **Improved Concentration and Reflexes**: Requires instant judgment and precise tapping, naturally training concentration and reflexes through daily play.
- **Brain Activation**: Quickly recognizing numbers and alphabets and thinking about the next move activates the brain, helping maintain and improve cognitive abilities.
- **Stress Relief and Change of Pace**: Easy to enjoy with simple controls, it helps relieve daily stress and refresh your mind.
- **Sense of Accomplishment and Self-Growth**: Aiming for high scores and higher ranks provides a sense of accomplishment and self-growth.

## Recommended for these people

- Those who want to improve concentration and memory with daily brain training.
- Those looking for fun games to train reflexes and quick thinking.
- Those who want a casual game to kill time during breaks.
- Those who enjoy competing with other players in rankings.
- Those who want to enjoy a simple yet profound game for a long time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 📢 The game makes sounds during play, can I turn them off?
You can toggle game sounds ON/OFF from the settings screen.

### 🌐 I don't want my name to appear in the ranking, is that possible?
You can play without entering a name. In that case, it will not be displayed in the ranking. Also, you can change your name anytime from the settings screen.

### 📺 Can I hide ads?
By using the in-app subscription, you can remove ads and enjoy the game more comfortably.

### 🗑️ I want to delete my account, what should I do?
You can proceed with the deletion from "Delete Account" in the settings screen. Please note that deleting your account will also delete your game progress and ranking data.

## Recommended Tap Number Settings

1. **Select Game Mode**: Choose either Number Mode or Alphabet Mode and start playing at your preferred difficulty.
2. **Sound Settings**: Set to OFF if you want to concentrate, or ON if you want to enjoy the game more.
3. **User Name Registration**: Participate in rankings and compete with players worldwide.
