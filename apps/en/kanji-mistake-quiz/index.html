Kanji Mistake Quiz: Boost Concentration with Brain Training Puzzles!

"Kanji Mistake Quiz" is a brain training puzzle game where you find the single different Kanji character among many that look identical at first glance. With its simple yet profound gameplay, it thoroughly trains your concentration and attention. This free puzzle game is perfect for students and commuters looking to kill time or activate their brains during short breaks.

Key Features: Diverse Modes to Stimulate Your Brain

  • Quick Game Mode: Short and quick brain training! Keep solving problems until time runs out and see how far you can go.
  • Continuous Game Mode: Time recovers with each problem solved! This mode allows you to clear more problems consecutively while maintaining concentration.
  • Endless Mode: No time limit, no mis-tap count! A hint function is also available, making it ideal for those who want to practice thoroughly or enjoy casually.
  • 5 Difficulty Levels: From "Very Easy" to "Very Difficult," we offer 5 difficulty levels that can be enjoyed by beginners and advanced players alike.
  • Ranking Function: Compete with players worldwide and test the results of your brain training! Comparing scores with friends and aiming for personal bests will keep you engaged for a long time.
  • Customization Function: By watching ads, you can freely set the theme color and font for one hour. Customize the game screen to your liking and enjoy playing even more.

Benefits of this app

  • Improved Attention and Concentration: Finding the mistake among similar Kanji characters enhances attention to detail and trains concentration.
  • Brain Activation: The joy of solving puzzles naturally stimulates the brain, leading to improved thinking and recognition abilities.
  • Enriched Spare Time: Effectively utilize short breaks, such as on trains or during rests, for easy brain training.

Recommended for these people!

  • Students and working adults looking for brain training games to kill time during commutes.
  • Those who want to improve their attention and concentration.
  • Those who like Kanji puzzles and character recognition games.
  • Those looking for free and casual puzzle games.
  • Those who want to train their brain in a fun, game-like way.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How can I display my name in the ranking?

A: Please register your name from the settings screen. You can play without entering a name, but it will not appear in the ranking.

Q: How can I change the theme and font?

A: You can change the settings from "Theme Settings" or "Font Settings" on the settings screen.

Q: What happens if I delete my account?

A: If you delete your account, all game results and ranking data will be deleted. Please be careful.

How to Set Up / How to Use

  1. Select Game Mode: Choose your preferred mode from "Quick Game," "Continuous Game," or "Training (Endless)" from the home screen.
  2. Select Difficulty: In each game mode, choose the level you want to challenge from 5 difficulty levels.
  3. Start Game: Tap the one different Kanji character from those displayed on the screen.
  4. Register Name: If you want to participate in the ranking, register your name on the settings screen.
  5. Customize: Change the theme and font on the settings screen to customize the game screen to your liking.
