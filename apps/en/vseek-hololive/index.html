Never Miss a Hololive Stream! YouTube Notifications & Schedule Manager – V-Seek

V-Seek is an unofficial app built for Hololive fans, providing YouTube stream notifications, schedule management, and video search.
With its lightweight performance and intuitive design, it makes supporting your oshi smarter and easier. From live streams and archives to highlights, shorts, and collab videos, V-Seek supports your entire Hololive YouTube experience.

Key Features: Enjoy Hololive YouTube Streams More Comfortably

  • Real-Time Stream Schedule Display: Instantly check the full Hololive YouTube schedule the moment you open the app. Smooth performance ensures stress-free use even during busy times.
  • Favorites & Custom Notifications: Register only your oshis and receive push notifications for surprise guerrilla streams or collabs. Unwanted notifications can be turned off for a personalized experience.
  • Video Filtering & Search: Switch between categories like “Live Streams,” “Archives,” “Clips,” and “Collabs.” Hide channels you’re not interested in for a cleaner list.
  • Simple, Intuitive Design: Switch stream types with a single tap. Thumbnails and channel images can be enlarged for detailed viewing.
  • Instant Push Notifications for Guerrilla Streams: Be notified in real-time when your oshi goes live—never miss an important moment again.
  • Channel Hiding: Hide individual channels to create your own customized list.
  • Video Quality Settings: Choose between performance priority, balanced, or high quality for the best viewing experience depending on your network.
  • Customizable Display Modes: Choose from Large Thumbnail, List, Compact, Compact 2-column, or Compact 3-column layouts.
  • Member-Only Video Display: Stay updated on members-only streams so you won’t miss exclusive content.

  • Image Zoom: Long-press thumbnails, channel icons, or banners to view them in detail.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

📢 How do I enable notifications?

Tap the bell icon on a channel and set it to “ON” to get notified right when streams start.

🎬 Can I hide channels I’m not interested in?

Yes. Go to Settings > Hide Channels to manage individually and keep only the ones you want.

🌐 Why do I see errors or outdated data?

The app uses the shared Holodex API by default, which has limits. Setting your own API key improves stability and ensures you always get the latest stream info. Go to Settings > Holodex API Key to set it up.

📺 How can I remove ads?

Ads support the app’s development, but you can remove them via in-app purchase for a smoother experience.

🔍 How do I zoom in on thumbnails or channel icons?

Simply long-press any thumbnail, channel icon, or banner image to enlarge it.

Setup & How to Use

  1. Download the App: Install “V-Seek” from the App Store.
  2. Register Your Oshis: Add your favorite Hololive talents to create your personalized timeline.
  3. Enable Notifications: Tap the bell icon on each channel to turn on live stream alerts.
  4. Customize the Display: Adjust video quality, layout style, and hidden channels in the settings.
  5. Optional: API Key Setup: For the best stability and fastest updates, set your own Holodex API key.
