Blackout Brain Training Puzzle: Tile Flipping Game for Dementia Prevention and Concentration Boost

"Blackout Brain Training Puzzle" is a tile-flipping game that offers deep strategy with simple rules. When you tap a white tile on the screen, that tile and the tiles above, below, left, and right of it simultaneously flip colors. If you blacken all the tiles, you clear the stage. Aim to clear in the minimum number of taps and enhance your thinking and concentration skills.

Key Features: Diverse Puzzle Experiences to Train Your Brain

  • Simple and intuitive controls: Easy to play with just a tap, anyone can enjoy the game immediately.
  • Tile-flipping puzzle: Unique gameplay where tapping a cell flips the colors of its adjacent cells (up, down, left, right).
  • Rich difficulty settings: With 5 levels of difficulty from very easy, easy, normal, difficult, to very difficult, it can be enjoyed by beginners and advanced players alike.
  • Record and challenge: Record your number of clears and best score (minimum taps) and challenge yourself to update your personal best.
  • Sound ON/OFF function: Convenient when you need to concentrate or play in public places.
  • Secure Privacy: Clearly states Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, also includes inquiry and share functions.

Benefits of this app: Easy brain power-up every day

  • Dementia prevention: Continuously playing a little each day supports brain activation and contributes to dementia prevention.
  • Improved logical thinking: By aiming for the minimum number of taps, you naturally train your logical thinking skills.
  • Boost concentration: Engaging intensely for short periods helps increase sustained concentration.
  • Effective use of spare time: Since it can be played in short bursts, you can easily train your brain during commutes, waiting times, or other small pockets of free time.
  • Stress relief: Immersing yourself in simple puzzles can help you forget daily stress and refresh your mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the goal of the game?

A: To tap white tiles and make all tiles black.

Q: Can I choose the difficulty level?

A: Yes, you can choose from 5 levels: very easy, easy, normal, difficult, and very difficult.

Q: Are there any tips for clearing with the minimum number of taps?

A: It's important to understand the characteristic that tapping a tile also flips its adjacent tiles (up, down, left, right), and to think strategically to blacken the entire board with fewer moves. Challenging yourself repeatedly to find patterns can also be helpful.

How to use Blackout Brain Training Puzzle

  1. Launch the app and select a difficulty level.
  2. Tap white tiles to flip their colors.
  3. Clear the stage when all tiles are black.
  4. You can toggle sound ON/OFF and other settings from the settings screen.
