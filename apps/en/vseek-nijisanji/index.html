Never Miss a Nijisanji Stream! Live & Schedule Notification App V-Seek

V-Seek is an unofficial app made for Nijisanji fans. It helps you quickly check livestreams and schedules, prevents you from missing streams with real-time notifications, and lets you search for highlights and collab videos. With smooth performance and an intuitive UI, it upgrades your Nijisanji life to be more comfortable and more fun.

  • Video Quality Settings: Choose from performance priority, balanced, or high quality to match your device and network.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

📢 How do I receive stream notifications?

Tap the bell icon on each channel and set it to “ON” to get notified right when streams start.

🎬 How can I hide channels I’m not interested in?

Go to Settings > Hide Channels and manage them individually. Keep only the channels you care about.

🌐 What should I do if I see errors?

By default, the app uses a shared Holodex API key, which has access limits. Setting your own Holodex API key will improve stability and data freshness. Go to Settings > Set Holodex API Key to configure it.

📺 I don’t like ads. Can I remove them?

Yes. Ads support the app, but you can remove them with an in-app purchase for a stress-free experience.

Recommended Settings for V-Seek

  1. Enable Notifications: Tap the bell icon on your oshi’s channel to ensure you receive stream alerts.
  2. Hide Channels: Filter out channels you’re not interested in and build your own custom stream list.
  3. Adjust Video Quality: Choose performance, balanced, or high quality depending on your network and device.
  4. Set a Holodex API Key: Improve stability and reduce errors by configuring your own API key.
