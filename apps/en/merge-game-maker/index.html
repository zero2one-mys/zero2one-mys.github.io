Merge Game Maker: Suika Game-like Custom Game Creation & Ranking Battles

"Merge Game Maker" is a new kind of free casual game where you can create and play merge puzzles, like the popular Suika Game, with your own original themes. In addition to various themes such as animals, numbers, and alphabets, you can register your own images to enjoy a completely original merge game. Compete with players nationwide and aim for the top of the rankings!

Key Features: Play Your Own Merge Game to the Fullest

  • Original Game Creation: Easily create a merge game by simply registering 11 of your favorite images. Surprise your friends with original games.
  • Diverse Theme Selection: Play with various preset themes such as "Planets," "Animals," "Food," "SNS," "Christmas," "Alphabet," "Numbers," "Ocean," and "Emojis."
  • Ranking Battles: Test your skills with the ranking feature, competing against players nationwide. Aim for the top in "Today's Ranking," "This Month's Ranking," and "All-Time Ranking."
  • Giant Merge Challenge: Features a "Giant Merge Challenge" mode where you can play with larger items than usual. Enjoy a different merge experience by casually challenging it (not reflected in rankings).
  • Player Name Customization: Freely set and change your player name displayed in the rankings.
  • Sound Settings: You can toggle game BGM and sound effects ON/OFF.
  • Multi-language Support: Supports multiple languages so players worldwide can enjoy.

Benefits of this app: Fulfilling Creativity and Competitive Spirit

  • Infinite Ways to Play: Since you can create games with your favorite images, you'll never get bored and can experience new merge games.

  • Exciting with Friends: Share your original games and compete for scores in rankings, enjoying it as a communication tool with friends.
  • Platform for Self-Expression: Reflect your individuality and taste in the game by selecting images and themes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I create an original game?

A: From the home screen, select "Create" from "Custom Game" and follow the instructions to register 11 images. You can also crop images by tapping them.

Q: I want to modify/delete registered images.

A: Long-press the corresponding image on the TOP screen to display modify/delete options.

Q: Can I hide ads?

A: Yes, you can remove ads with in-app purchases for a more comfortable gaming experience.

Q: I want to change the player name displayed in the ranking.

A: Select "Change Player Name" from the settings screen, enter your new player name, and register it.

How to Set Up / How to Use

  1. Launch the app: After the splash screen appears, the game will start.
  2. Register Player Name: Register your player name at first launch or from the settings screen to participate in the ranking.
  3. Select Game Mode: Select normal mode from "Start" or "Giant Merge Challenge" to start playing.
  4. Select Theme: Choose an existing theme from "Custom Game" or create a new theme to play.
  5. Sound Settings: You can toggle BGM and sound effects ON/OFF from the settings screen.

Notes / Disclaimer

This app is free to enjoy, but in-app ads will be displayed. A subscription purchase is required to hide ads.
