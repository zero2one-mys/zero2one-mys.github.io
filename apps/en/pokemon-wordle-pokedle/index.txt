## Pokedle: Pokémon Name Guessing Quiz Game for Brain Training! Daily Challenges & Ranking Battles

**Pokedle** is a free Pokémon name guessing quiz game for fans.  
Test your Pokémon knowledge with simple yet deep gameplay mechanics. Play fresh **daily challenges**, compete in **national rankings**, and enjoy an addictive brain-training puzzle that’s perfect for commuting, school breaks, or relaxing at home.  

---

## ⚠ Important Note: Pokémon Names Must Be Entered in Japanese

Currently, this app only supports **Japanese Pokémon names** for answers.  
If you are playing outside Japan, you will need to input names in Japanese (Katakana or Hiragana as used in the official Japanese Pokémon names).  
This adds an extra challenge for international players and helps you learn the original Japanese names of your favorite Pokémon!  

---

## Key Features: Deepen Your Knowledge with Pokémon Name Guessing

- **Daily Challenge**  
  A new Pokémon is given every day. You have up to 10 tries to guess the correct name. Enjoy a fresh challenge daily.  

- **Version Selection Mode**  
  Choose which generation of Pokémon to play with. Focus on one era or aim to conquer all generations.  

- **Letter-by-letter hint system**  
  When you type a name, hints appear for each letter:  
  - **Green**: Correct letter in the correct position  
  - **Yellow**: Letter exists but in a different position  
  - **Gray**: The letter does not exist in the name  
  Use these clues to find the correct Pokémon name.  

- **Global Ranking System**  
  Compete against players across the country by solving as many challenges as possible. Prove your Pokémon knowledge by climbing the leaderboard!  

- **Title System**  
  Aim to conquer all Pokémon and earn special titles as a sign of your dedication.  

## Benefits of Playing This App

- **Brain Training**  
  Guessing Pokémon names strengthens memory and logical thinking.  

- **Perfect for Short Breaks**  
  Quick and easy gameplay makes it ideal for commutes, school breaks, or waiting times.  

- **Sense of Achievement**  
  Feel the satisfaction of completing daily challenges, conquering all Pokémon, or ranking high on the leaderboard.  

- **Community Connection**  
  Share your progress and rankings with friends and other players.  

## Recommended For

- Pokémon fans confident in their knowledge  
- People who want to enjoy quizzes with friends or family  
- Puzzle & brain-training game lovers  
- Players who like daily challenges and gradual progress  
- Anyone looking for a fun way to use spare time during travel or breaks  

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do I play?  
A: Guess the Pokémon’s name within 10 tries. Use the letter hints to guide your answer. *(Remember: answers must be in Japanese!)*  

### Q: How do I join the ranking system?  
A: Register your player name in the app’s settings. You can still play without registering.  

### Q: Are there ads?  
A: Yes, the app is supported by ads, but you can remove them with a subscription.  

### Q: Can I change the theme or font?  
A: Yes, from the settings menu. Watching an ad will unlock customization for 1 hour.  

## How to Play / Setup

1. **Select Game Mode**: Choose from “Quick Game,” “Continuous Game,” or “Training.”  
2. **Register Your Name**: Required only if you want to join the rankings.  
3. **Customize Themes & Fonts**: Adjust your visual preferences in settings.
