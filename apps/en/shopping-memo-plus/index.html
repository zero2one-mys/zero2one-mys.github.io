Shopping Memo+: Shopping List & Calculator App for Housewives

Daily shopping at supermarkets and drugstores, writing lists, and calculating totals can be surprisingly troublesome. "Shopping Memo+" is a convenient app that integrates a shopping list and calculator. It helps you shop efficiently within your budget while preventing forgotten or duplicate purchases. With category classification and list reordering functions, busy housewives can neatly shorten their shopping time.

Key Features: Make Daily Shopping Easier

  • Shopping list management

    • Quickly register necessary items and create a list
    • Check off purchased items with a single tap
    • Reorder the list to match the store's layout
    • "Undo" and "Redo" functions for peace of mind if you make a mistake

  • Smooth family shopping with category division

    • Freely classify items into "Groceries," "Daily Necessities," "Children's Goods," etc.
    • Create separate lists for each supermarket or drugstore
    • Organize by displaying frequently used categories at the top for easy viewing

  • Easy budget management with calculator function

    • Instantly calculate the total amount while shopping
    • One-tap consumption tax calculation with "+8%" and "+10%" buttons
    • Recalculate from calculation history, useful for household budgeting

  • Customize the look to your liking

    • Freely choose color themes
    • Supports eye-friendly dark mode

Benefits of this app

  • Prevents forgotten and duplicate purchases
  • ** 安心できる予算管理** (Secure budget management)
  • Smooth movement in the store
  • Simple and intuitive operation
  • Leads to savings & household budget management

Highly Recommended for

  • Housewives who want to manage family groceries and daily necessities collectively
  • Those who want to reduce forgotten purchases and wasteful spending
  • Those who frequently use a calculator while shopping
  • Those who want to categorize lists for efficient shopping
  • Those looking for a simple and cute design app

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I share my shopping list with my family?

A: Yes, there is a list sharing function. It's convenient when spouses or family members share shopping duties.

Q: Can I remove ads?

A: Yes, you can hide ads with an in-app purchase.

Q: Is there a limit to the number of categories?

A: There is no limit. You can freely add and manage them as needed.

Recommended Usage

  1. Customize categories for your family: Organize groceries, daily necessities, children's goods, etc., according to your household.
  2. Reorder list by store layout: Arranging items by supermarket aisle makes shopping even smoother.
  3. Select your favorite theme: Change the look and enjoy using it to match your mood.
