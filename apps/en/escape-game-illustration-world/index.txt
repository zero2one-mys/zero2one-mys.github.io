## Escape Game Illustration World: Cute Puzzle Game for Beginners

"Escape Game Illustration World" is a gentle puzzle escape game for beginners where you help a girl trapped in a cute illustrated world. No complicated operations are needed. Collect items, combine them, and solve hidden mysteries with just taps and swipes. It's a perfect free game for killing time, training your brain while enjoying a heartwarming story.

- **Escape from a cute illustrated mysterious world**
  Advance the story in a world drawn with captivating illustrations.
- **Gentle puzzle difficulty for beginners**
  Designed so that even those new to escape games can enjoy it to the end with peace of mind.
- **Collect, use, and combine items**
  Tap interesting places to find items, and sometimes combine them with other items for new discoveries.
- **Hint function for when you're stuck**
  If you can't solve a mystery, use the hint function to smoothly proceed.
- **Auto-save function to resume anytime**
  Your play progress is automatically saved, so you can resume playing immediately even if you interrupt it.
- **Customize to your liking with theme color changes**
  Choose your favorite from a rich selection of theme colors to change the app's atmosphere. Customizable for 1 hour with ad viewing, or always with a subscription.

  A subscription to remove in-app ads is also available for a more comfortable play experience.

  You can introduce the game to your friends with the text "Escape Game Illustration World | Enjoy the illustrated world while solving mysteries!"
