Escape Game Illustration World: Cute Puzzle Game for Beginners

"Escape Game Illustration World" is a gentle puzzle escape game for beginners where you help a girl trapped in a cute illustrated world. No complicated operations are needed. Collect items, combine them, and solve hidden mysteries with just taps and swipes. It's a perfect free game for killing time, training your brain while enjoying a heartwarming story.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

📢 Is there a hint function?

Yes, if you're really stuck, you can use the hint function to progress through the mystery.

🎨 Can I change the app's theme color?

Yes, you can change to your preferred theme color from the settings screen.

💰 Can I hide ads?

Yes, by subscribing to the in-app subscription, you can hide ads.

💾 Is play data saved?

Yes, play data is automatically saved by the auto-save function, so you can interrupt and resume anytime.

How to Set Up / How to Use

  1. Tap the screen to investigate interesting places and find hidden items and mysteries.
  2. Once you find an item, use it or combine it with other items to find clues to solve the mystery.
  3. If you encounter a mystery you can't solve, check the hint button on the screen to get help for the next step.
  4. Solve all the mysteries and safely escape the girl trapped in the cute illustrated world!
