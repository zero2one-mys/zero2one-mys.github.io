Microwave Time Calculator: Shorten Microwave Heating Time! Optimal Heating for Frozen Foods & Bento

"Microwave Time Calculator" is a convenient app that automatically converts the wattage and heating time listed on recipes or food packages to the optimal heating time for your microwave. No more failures like "overheated" or "still cold." It supports your busy daily life and makes daily meal preparation smart.

Key Features: Easier and More Accurate Microwave Cooking

  • Automatic heating time calculation by wattage conversion Simply input the "original wattage" and "original heating time," and it instantly calculates the optimal heating time for your microwave's wattage.
  • Supports major wattages & registers home microwave Supports major wattages such as 500W, 600W, 700W, and 800W. Furthermore, you can freely register your home microwave's wattage, making it usable in any environment.
  • Accurate calculation in minutes and seconds Fine time adjustment is possible, achieving optimal heating tailored to the characteristics of the ingredients.
  • Simple and intuitive UI design An easy-to-understand and user-friendly design that anyone can use without hesitation. Access necessary information with a single tap.
  • Customize to your liking with theme settings Choose your favorite color from various themes such as default, dark mode, red, pink, purple. Premium members can always change themes without ads.
  • Ad-free function (Premium member) Remove in-app ads with a subscription for a more comfortable experience.

Benefits of this app: Both time-saving and delicious

  • Just the right heating time without waste Prevents overheating or keeping ingredients cold, warming them to a delicious state every time.
  • Shorten cooking time in busy daily life Eliminates the hassle of calculations, significantly speeding up the preparation of frozen foods and convenience store bentos.
  • Reduce cooking failures and save on food expenses You can enjoy your food without waste, eating it deliciously.

How to Set Up / How to Use

  1. Launch the app and set the "original wattage" and "original heating time."
  2. Select your microwave's wattage under "Microwave Wattage."
  3. The "heating time" will be automatically calculated and displayed.
  4. You can change the app's theme and check the privacy policy from the settings screen.

Notes / Disclaimer

This app is a tool to assist with microwave heating times and does not guarantee cooking results. Optimal heating times may vary depending on the type and condition of the ingredients and individual microwave differences.
