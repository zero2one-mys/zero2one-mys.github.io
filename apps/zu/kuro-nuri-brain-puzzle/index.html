I-Blackout Brain Training Puzzle: Umdlalo wokuguqula amathayela wokuvimbela i-dementia nokukhulisa ukugxila

"I-Blackout Brain Training Puzzle" ngumdlalo wokuguqula amathayela onikeza isu elijulile ngemithetho elula. Uma uthepha ithayela elimhlophe esikrinini, lelo thayela namathayela aphezulu, ngezansi, kwesokunxele, nangesokudla kulo aguqula imibala ngasikhathi sinye. Uma uwafaka wonke amathayela umbala omnyama, uyawina isiteji. Zama ukuwina ngamanani amancane okuthepha futhi uthuthukise amakhono akho okucabanga nokugxila.

Izici Eziyinhloko: Okuhlangenwe nakho okuhlukahlukene kwe-puzzle ukuqeqesha ubuchopho bakho

  • Izilawuli ezilula neziqondakalayo: Kulula ukudlala ngokuthepha nje, noma ubani angajabulela umdlalo ngokushesha.
  • I-puzzle yokuguqula amathayela: I-gameplay eyingqayizivele lapho ukuthepha iseli kuguqula imibala yamaseli ayo aseduze (phezulu, phansi, kwesokunxele, kwesokudla).
  • Izilungiselelo zobunzima obucebile: Ngamazinga ama-5 obunzima kusukela kokulula kakhulu, okulula, okujwayelekile, okunzima, kuye kokunzima kakhulu, kungajatshulelwa abaqalayo nabadlali abathuthukile ngokufanayo.
  • Qopha futhi uzibandakanye: Qopha inani lakho lokugcina kanye namaphuzu aphezulu (okuncane ukuthepha) bese uzibandakanya ukuze ubuyekeze okungcono kwakho.
  • Umsebenzi we-ON/OFF womsindo: Kulungele lapho udinga ukugxila noma ukudlala ezindaweni zomphakathi.
  • Ubumfihlo Obuvikelekile: Kukhombisa ngokucacile Inqubomgomo Yobumfihlo kanye Nemigomo Yokusebenzisa, kufaka phakathi imibuzo nemisebenzi yokwabelana.

Izinzuzo zalolu hlelo lokusebenza: Ukukhulisa amandla obuchopho kalula nsuku zonke

  • Ukuvimbela i-dementia: Ukudlala kancane nsuku zonke kusekela ukusebenza kobuchopho futhi kufaka isandla ekuvikeleni i-dementia.
  • Ukuthuthukiswa kokucabanga okunengqondo: Ngokuzama ukuthola inani elincane lokuthepha, uqeqesha ngokwemvelo amakhono akho okucabanga okunengqondo.
  • Khulisa ukugxila: Ukuzibandakanya kakhulu isikhathi esifushane kusiza ukukhulisa ukugxila okuqhubekayo.
  • Ukusetshenziswa okusebenzayo kwesikhathi esikhululekile: Njengoba kungadlalwa ngesikhathi esifushane, ungaqeqesha kalula ubuchopho bakho ngesikhathi sokuhamba, izikhathi zokulinda, noma ezinye izikhathi ezincane zesikhathi esikhululekile.
  • Ukukhululeka ekucindezelekeni: Ukuzicwilisa kuma-puzzle alula kungakusiza ukhohlwe ukucindezeleka kwansuku zonke futhi uvuselele ingqondo yakho.

Kunconywa ngalaba bantu!

  • Labo abaqaphela ukuvimbela i-dementia nokugcina impilo yobuchopho.
  • Labo abafuna ukujabulela ukuqeqeshwa kobuchopho okungajwayelekile nemidlalo ye-puzzle ngesikhathi sabo samahhala.
  • Abantu abaneminyaka ephakathi nendawo nabadala abangahleli kahle ngemisebenzi eyinkimbinkimbi.
  • Labo abafuna ukuthuthukisa ukucabanga kwabo okunengqondo nokugxila.
  • Labo abafuna umdlalo we-puzzle olula kodwa ojulile.

Imibuzo Ebuzwa Njalo

Q: Iyini inhloso yalo mdlalo?

A: Ukuthepha amathayela amhlophe futhi wenze wonke amathayela abe mnyama.

Q: Ngingakhetha izinga lobunzima?

A: Yebo, ungakhetha kumazinga ama-5: okulula kakhulu, okulula, okujwayelekile, okunzima, nokunzima kakhulu.

Q: Ingabe kukhona amathiphu okwenza kube lula ngamanani amancane okuthepha?

A: Kubalulekile ukuqonda isici sokuthi ukuthepha ithayela nakho kuguqula amathayela ayo aseduze (phezulu, phansi, kwesokunxele, kwesokudla), nokucabanga ngesu ukuze kwenziwe mnyama lonke ibhodi ngamanani amancane okuhamba. Ukuzibandakanya kaningi ukuze uthole amaphethini nakho kungasiza.

Ungayisebenzisa Kanjani I-Blackout Brain Training Puzzle

  1. Qala uhlelo lokusebenza bese ukhetha izinga lobunzima.
  2. Thepha amathayela amhlophe ukuze uguqule imibala yawo.
  3. Wina isiteji lapho wonke amathayela esemnyama.
  4. Ungashintsha umsindo ON/OFF nezinye izilungiselelo kusuka esikrinini sezilungiselelo.
