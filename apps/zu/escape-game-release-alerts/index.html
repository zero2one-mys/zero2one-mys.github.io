  • Sesha & Hlunga: Sesha kalula izikhishwe ezintsha ngokucacisa izimo ezifana negama lomdlalo, uhlobo, usuku lokukhishwa, kanye nokuthi mahhala/ukhokhelwe.
  • Ukuphathwa Komlando Wokudlala: Rekhoda imidlalo osuvele wayidlala, okwenza kube lula ukukhetha umdlalo olandelayo ozowudlala.
  • Umsebenzi Wokusetha Itimu: Setha itimu oyithandayo ngemodi emnyama/emhlophe.
  • Ukusekelwa Kwezilimi Eziningi: Ungahlola futhi ulwazi mayelana nama-escape game avela phesheya.
  • Ukubonisa Izikhangiso kanye Nohlelo Olukhokhelwayo Olungenazikhangiso: Imisebenzi eyisisekelo iyatholakala mahhala. Uhlelo olukhokhelwayo likuvumela ukuthi ufihle izikhangiso ngokuphelele.

Ungahlola imidlalo edumile futhi ekalwe kakhulu kumsebenzi wesilinganiso, noma useshe ngokucacisa izimo ezifana negama lomdlalo, uhlobo, nosuku lokukhishwa ngomsebenzi wokusesha nokuhlunga.

💰 Ngingalusebenzisa mahhala uhlelo lokusebenza?

Imisebenzi eyisisekelo iyatholakala mahhala. Uhlelo olukhokhelwayo lufaka phakathi izinzuzo ezifana nokufihla izikhangiso ngokuphelele.
