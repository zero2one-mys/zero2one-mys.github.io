## I-Ehomaki Compass & Omikuji Fortune: Uhlelo Lokusebenza Olunenhlanhla lweSetsubun kanye Nempilo Yansuku Zonke

**I-Ehomaki Compass & Omikuji Fortune** wuhlelo lokusebenza olujabulisayo oluhlanganisa izinto ezimbili ezithandekayo zamasiko aseJapane:
- **Ikhampasi** ekuqondisa endleleni efanele yenhlahla (Eho) yokudla umqulu wakho we-sushi *Ehomaki* ngesikhathi se-*Setsubun* (umkhosi wesizini waseJapane).
- **Inhlanhla yendabuko ye-Omikuji** esekelwe ku-"Gansan Daishi Hyakusen" yasemlandweni, esetshenziswa emathempelini afana ne-Asakusa ne-Enryakuji.

Ngomklamo wayo omuhle wabalingiswa kanye nesixhumi esibonakalayo esilula, esinembile, uhlelo lokusebenza lwenza kube lula kunoma ubani—izingane, abantu abadala, nemindeni—ukujabulela amasiko aseJapane ngenkathi futhi behlola inhlanhla yabo yansuku zonke.

## Izici Ezibalulekile: Uhlelo Lwakho Lokusebenza Lwenhlanhla lwaseJapane Oluphelele

- **I-Ehomaki Compass**: Ibonisa isiqondiso esinenhlanhla esifanele (*Eho*) seSetsubun minyaka yonke. Khomba ifoni yakho njengekhampasi ukuze ujabulele umqulu wakho we-Ehomaki ubheke endleleni efanele.
- **Inhlanhla yangempela ye-Omikuji**: Jabulela i-"Gansan Daishi Omikuji" yendabuko, kusukela ku-*Daikichi* (Isibusiso Esikhulu) kuya ku-*Kyo* (Ukungalungi). Ilungele ukuhlola inhlanhla yansuku zonke ngokushesha.
- **Ukwehlukanisa Itimu**: Yenza uhlelo lokusebenza lube ngelakho ngezindikimba zemibala ehlukene ukuze luhambisane nokunambitha kwakho.
- **Imininingwane Yamasiko e-Ehomaki**: Funda ngemvelaphi, izithako, nomlando we-Ehomaki ngama-trivia ajabulisayo agqugquzelwe amasiko nomlando waseJapane.
- **Umdlalo wephazili onenhlanhla**: Phendula amathayela phakathi kwe-"Oni" (idemoni) ne-"Fuku" (inhlanhla enhle) emdlalweni wephazili oyingqayizivele wokuqeqesha ubuchopho.
- **Ukwabelana Okulula**: Yabelana ngenhlanhla yakho nokuzijabulisa kweSetsubun nabangani nomndeni ku-LINE, X (phambilini eyi-Twitter), namanye amapulatifomu okuxhumana nabantu.
- **Ukusekela & Izinqubomgomo**: Finyelela izinqubomgomo zobumfihlo, imigomo yokusebenzisa, nefomu lokuxhumana elilula noma nini.
- **Izinhlelo zokusebenza Ezinconyiwe**: Zitholele izinhlelo zokusebenza eziningi zamasiko aseJapane kanye nezinhlelo zokusebenza ezinethimu yenhlanhla ezivela kunjiniyela ofanayo.

- **Funda amasiko aseJapane**: Julekisa ukuqonda kwakho iSetsubun, Ehomaki, ne-Omikuji ngenkathi ujabule.

### 📢 Ingabe isiqondiso esinenhlanhla (*Eho*) siyashintsha minyaka yonke?
Yebo. Isiqondiso esinenhlanhla se-Ehomaki siyashintsha minyaka yonke, futhi uhlelo lokusebenza luvuselela ngokuzenzakalelayo ngendlela efanele.

### 🧭 Ikhampasi ayibonisi indlela efanele.
Kwamanye amadivayisi e-Android, ukunemba kwenzwa kungahluka. Sicela ubambe ifoni yakho iyisicaba bese uyiqhathanisa nolunye uhlelo lokusebenza lwekhampasi uma kudingeka.

### 🏮 Ngingakwazi ukubhula i-Omikuji nsuku zonke?
Yebo, ungabhula i-Omikuji kanye ngosuku ukuze uhlole inhlanhla yakho yansuku zonke.

Thepha ithayela ukuze uphendule amathayela azungezile phakathi kwe-"Fuku" (inhlanhla) ne-"Oni" (idemoni). Inhloso ukuguqula wonke amathayela abe "Fuku."
