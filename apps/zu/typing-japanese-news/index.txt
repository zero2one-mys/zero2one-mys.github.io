## Izici Eziyinhloko: Ukufunda Ulimi Kuhlangana Nokuzijwayeza Ukuthayipha

* **Zijwayeze Ngezihloko Ezihlukahlukene Zezindaba**
  Hlola izigaba ezifana nezindaba ezihamba phambili, umphakathi, ezamazwe ngamazwe, ezepolitiki, ezomnotho, isayensi, indlela yokuphila, ezokuzijabulisa, nemidlalo. Khetha izindaba ezikuthakazelisayo futhi zijwayeze ukuthayipha ngenkathi ufunda amagama amasha kumongo.

* **Landelela Inqubekelaphambili Yakho Ngamaphuzu Nezikhundla**
  Uhlelo lokusebenza luqopha isivinini sakho sokuthayipha (izinhlamvu ngomzuzu) nokunemba ngokuzenzakalelayo, lubonise lokhu kumagrafu afundeka kalula. Ungabona intuthuko yakho yansuku zonke futhi uhlale unentshisekelo. Bhalisa igama lomdlali ukuze ujoyine izikhundla zansuku zonke, zanyanga zonke, nezazo zonke izikhathi futhi uqhathanise nabanye abafundi.

* **Lalela Ngenkathi Uthayipha (Ukusekelwa kwe-TTS)**
  Izindaba zingafundwa ngokuzwakalayo ngosizo lwe-text-to-speech, okukuvumela ukuthi uzijwayeze ukuthayipha nokuqonda ukulalela ngesikhathi esifanayo. Kuhle kakhulu ekuthuthukiseni ukuqwashisa ngokuphimisela namakhono wokulalela esiJapane jikelele.

* **Ukusekelwa Okugcwele Kokufaka KwesiJapane (Ukuguqulwa Kwe-Kana)**
  Zijwayeze ukuthayipha isiJapane ngokuguqulwa okufanele kwe-kana-to-kanji, njengoba nje ungasebenzisa ikhibhodi yangempela yesiJapane. Lokhu kusiza kakhulu abafundi abahlose ukufunda amakhono okufaka anembile.

* **Izilungiselelo Zomsindo Ezifundekayo Nokususwa Kwezikhangiso**
  Vula/vala imisindo yokuthayipha nomculo wangemuva. Ukuthenga okungaphakathi nohlelo okungakhethwa kukuvumela ukuthi ususe izikhangiso ukuze uthole ulwazi lokufunda olugxile ngokugcwele.

* **Multi-Platform, Multi-Language**
  Kuyatholakala ku-iOS, Android, Mac, naku-Apple Vision. Uhlelo lokusebenza lusekela nezilimi eziningi ukuze lusize abafundi abaningi emhlabeni jikelele.

* **Thuthukisa Ukuthayipha Ngenkathi Ufunda Amagama**

  Kufanelekile ekuhambeni, ekulindeni kulayini, noma kunoma yisiphi isikhathi sokuphumula esifushane—guqula imizuzu eyonakele ibe izikhathi zokufunda eziphumelelayo.
