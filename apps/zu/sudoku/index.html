Sudoku: Qeqesha Ubuchopho Bakho Ngezindida ze-Sudoku futhi Uthuthukise Ukucabanga Okunengqondo

"Sudoku" uhlelo lokusebenza olugcwele lokuqeqesha ubuchopho oluhlanganisa izinkinga ze-Sudoku ezingaphezu kuka-20,000 nezinga lobunzima obuyi-7. Kusukela kubaqalayo kuya kubadlali abaphambili abafuna ukuzama izinkinga ezinzima, wonke umuntu angakujabulela. Khuthaza ukucabanga kwakho nezinselele zansuku zonke bese uqala umkhuba wakho wansuku zonke wokuvuselela ubuchopho.

Izici Eziyinhloko: Imisebenzi Ebanzi Yokudlala I-Sudoku Ngokukhululeka

  • Izinkinga ze-Sudoku ezingaphezu kuka-20,000: Amazinga obunzima ayi-7 kusukela "Kulula Kakhulu" kuya "Enkingeni Enzima Kakhulu" ayatholakala. Ungaziphonsela inselelo ngokuya ngezinga lakho.
  • Inselele Yansuku Zonke: Izinkinga ezintsha zibuyekezwa nsuku zonke ukuze ubuchopho bakho buhlale busebenza ngaphandle kokudina.
  • Imisebenzi esizayo elula:
    • Umsebenzi wememo: Ungabhala phansi izinombolo ezingaba khona, uqinisekise ukuthula kwengqondo ngisho nasezimweni eziyinkimbinkimbi.
    • Umsebenzi wememo ozenzakalelayo: Ubonisa ngokuzenzakalelayo izinombolo ezingaba khona ngaphandle kokuhlupheka, usekela ukucabanga kwakho.
    • Umsebenzi wokusikisela: Uphakamisa umnyakazo olandelayo lapho ubambekile, ukusiza ukuthi uqhubeke ngaphandle kwezinkinga.
    • Umsebenzi we-UNDO/REDO: Ungazama ngokukhululeka ngaphandle kokukhathazeka ngamaphutha.
    • Umsebenzi wokusula inombolo: Sula kalula izinombolo ezingadingekile.
  • Ukubhalisa okungenazikhangiso: Kufihla izikhangiso eziphazamisayo, kunikeza indawo yokudlala enethezekile.
  • Umsindo ON/OFF: Shintsha ngokukhululeka phakathi kokucisha lapho ufuna ukugxilisa ingqondo futhi uvule lapho ufuna ukuphumula.
  • Ukufinyelela olwazini: Ungakusebenzisa ngokuthula kwengqondo ngezinqubomgomo zobumfihlo, imigomo yokusebenzisa, imibuzo, nomsebenzi wokwabelana ngohlelo lokusebenza.

Izinzuzo zalolu hlelo lokusebenza

  • Ukucabanga okunengqondo okuthuthukisiwe: Ngokusebenzisa inqubo yokuxazulula i-Sudoku, amakhono okucabanga okunengqondo athuthukiswa ngokwemvelo.
  • Ukuvimbela i-dementia: Ukusebenzisa ubuchopho bakho njalo kuyabuvuselela futhi kunomthelela ekuvikeleni i-dementia.
  • Thuthukisa ukugxilisa ingqondo: Isikhathi esichithwa ekuzindleni ngezindida sithuthukisa ukugxilisa ingqondo kwansuku zonke.
  • Ukukhulula ukucindezeleka nokuphumula: Ukuzibandakanya ngokuthula ezindida kuholela ekuthuleni kwengqondo nasekuvuseleleni.
  • Umkhuba wansuku zonke wokuqeqesha ubuchopho: Ngenxa yezinselele zansuku zonke, ungakwenza kalula ukuqeqesha ubuchopho umkhuba.

Kunconywa kulaba bantu!

  • Abadala abafuna ukuqeqesha ubuchopho babo: Labo abanentshisekelo ekuvikeleni i-dementia nasekusebenziseni ubuchopho.
  • Labo abafuna ukuqeqesha ubuchopho ngesikhathi sohambo/amakhefu esikole: Labo abafuna ukusebenzisa ubuchopho babo kalula ngesikhathi esifushane.
  • Abadlali abaphambili abafuna ukuzama "i-Sudoku enzima kakhulu!": Abathandi bezindida abafuna ukuzama izinkinga eziningi ezinzima.
  • Labo abafuna ukubulala isikhathi ngemidlalo yezindida: Labo abafuna ukusebenzisa isikhathi sabo ngempumelelo ngemidlalo ye-Sudoku esezingeni eliphezulu.
  • Labo abafuna ukuthuthukisa ukucabanga okunengqondo nokugxilisa ingqondo: Labo abahlose ukuthuthukisa ukusebenza empilweni yansuku zonke nasemsebenzini.

Imibuzo Ebuzwa Njalo

Q. Ngingakwazi yini ukufihla izikhangiso kuhlelo lokusebenza?

A. Yebo, ungakwazi ukufihla izikhangiso ngokusebenzisa ukubhalisa kwangaphakathi nohlelo.

Q. Mangaki amazinga obunzima akhona?

A. Kukhona amazinga obunzima ayi-7 kusukela "Kulula Kakhulu" kuya "Enkingeni Enzima Kakhulu." Kungajatshulelwa abaqalayo nabadlali abaphambili ngokufanayo.

Q. Ingabe izinkinga ezintsha zihlinzekwa nsuku zonke?

A. Yebo, izinkinga ezintsha ze-Sudoku zibuyekezwa nsuku zonke njenge-"Izinselele Zansuku Zonke."

Indlela Yokumisa / Indlela Yokusebenzisa

  1. Esikrinini sasekhaya, khetha "Umdlalo Omusha" noma "Inselele Yansuku Zonke."
  2. Khetha izinga lobunzima bese uqala indida.
  3. Gcwalisa igridi engu-9x9 ngenombolo 1-9 ukuze kungabikho nombolo ephindaphindwayo kunoma yimuphi umugqa, ikholomu, noma ibhulokhi engu-3x3.
  4. Uma ubambekile, sebenzisa "Amacebo" kanye "Nomsebenzi wememo."
  5. Ungasebenzisa izinkinobho ezifana ne-"Hlehlisa," "Yenza futhi," "Sula," "Memo," "Isikiselo," kanye "Nememo Ezenzakalelayo" ezansi kwesikrini.
  6. Esikrinini sezilungiselelo, ungashintsha umsindo ON/OFF bese uhlola inqubomgomo yobumfihlo.
